AetherEngine User Manual & Tutorial

Learn how to master entity vision cones, combat overlaps, menu state transitions, and ship your first offline-playable top-down game.

1. Core Concepts & Entity Classes

PLAYER:

The playable character controlled with WASD or Arrow Keys. Tracks HP, Coins, and XP leveling progression.

NPC:

Non-player characters with adjustable 4-directional vision cones. Displays custom dialogue modals when approached.

ENEMY:

Hostile entities. Can chase the player on detection and deal contact damage upon collision overlap.

ITEM:

Pickup objects that immediately award Coins, Health restores, or Experience points when touched.

2. Step-by-Step Tutorial: Building Your First Mini-RPG

Step 1: Laying Out the Map

Open the . Click PLAYER in the left palette and place your player character near the left edge of the grid canvas. Then, select LANDSCAPE or PROP and build border walls or obstacle rocks around the level perimeter. Ensure "Solid Barrier" is checked in the Inspector for wall obstacles.

Step 2: Adding an NPC Questgiver

Click NPC in the palette and place one near the starting area. Select the NPC to open the Inspector panel. Set North, South, East, and West vision distances to 80px. Type a quest dialogue line in the dialogue text box: "Beware of the goblin guarding the chest! Collect coins to level up!"

Step 3: Creating Items & Level Up Rewards

Place several ITEM entities along the dungeon path. Select an item and configure it to give +50 XP and +10 Coins. When the player collects enough XP to reach the threshold (e.g. 100 XP), a level-up reward selection modal will appear automatically during gameplay!

Step 4: Setting Up Enemy Patrols & Vision

Place an ENEMY near the dungeon exit. Set its movement behavior to Move To / Chase Player and set East/West vision ranges to 150px. Set Enemy HP to 30 and Overlap Damage to 15.

Step 5: Testing & Offline Playtesting

Click ▶ Launch Game in New Tab in the Studio header. A new browser tab will launch containing your compiled game running inside a Blob URL sandbox. Use WASD or Arrow Keys to move, press ESC to pause, and test all mechanics!

🤖 BYO-AI Key Configuration

Provide your Gemini API key to unlock automated dialogue writing and procedural lore generation in the Inspector panel. Key is saved locally in your browser.
