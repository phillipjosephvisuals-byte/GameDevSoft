AetherEngine User Manual & Tutorial

Learn how to master entity vision cones, RPG leveling formulas, custom pop-up modals, ending triggers, and ship your first offline-playable top-down game.

1. Core Concepts & Entity Classes

PLAYER:

The main character controlled with WASD or Arrow Keys. Tracks HP, Coins, and XP leveling progression.

NPC:

Non-player characters with adjustable 4-directional vision cones. Displays custom dialogue modals when approached.

ENEMY:

Hostile entities. Can chase the player on vision detection and deal contact damage upon collision overlap.

ITEM:

Pickup objects that immediately award Coins, Health restores, or Experience points when touched.

PROP:

Decorative map elements such as trees, signs, chests, or furniture with optional solid collision.

LANDSCAPE:

Solid barriers, rocks, wall tiles, or boundaries that block movement for players and enemies.

2. XP Formulas, Custom Modals & Ending Triggers

⭐ Leveling & XP Rules:

Set base Level 1 XP requirements and multiplier curves (e.g. 1.5x per level). Automatically pause gameplay on level up to present customized perk selections.

🪟 Custom Pop-Up Builder:

Design custom on-screen pop-up windows in the left sidebar. Add interactive buttons carrying action payloads like boosting player Max HP (+20) or Attack (+5).

🏆 Game-Ending Triggers:

Choose rule-based win conditions (Collect Coins, Reach Player Level, Defeat All Enemies) and set target numeric thresholds that automatically display the Victory screen.

3. Step-by-Step Tutorial: Building Your First Mini-RPG

Step 1: Map Layout & Boundary Walls

Open the . Click PLAYER in the left palette and click near the left side of the grid canvas to place your hero. Next, select LANDSCAPE and build boundary walls along the edge of the grid. Ensure Solid Barrier is checked in the Inspector for each wall block.

Step 2: Placing NPCs & Configuring Vision Cones

Select NPC from the palette and place an guide character near your player. Click the NPC to open the Inspector panel. Set North, South, East, and West vision distances to 80px. In the dialogue text box, type: "Welcome adventurer! Collect 30 coins to complete your quest!"

Step 3: Configuring XP Leveling & Items

In the left sidebar under XP Leveling Rules, set Base XP to 100 and Multiplier to 1.5. Select ITEM in the palette and place several coin bags on the grid. Set their pickup rewards in the Inspector panel to +30 Coins and +100 XP.

Step 4: Setting Up Custom Pop-Up Modals

Look at the Custom Pop-Up Modals section in the left sidebar. Notice the pre-built level up modal titled "Level Up Perk Selection!". You can click + New to create additional custom pop-ups with custom text and interactive stat boost buttons (+20 HP or +5 Attack).

Step 5: Enemy Combat & Victory Triggers

Place an ENEMY guard near the center room. Set its behavior to Chase Player, set its Vision Range to 120px, and give it 15 overlap damage. Then, under Victory & Ending Triggers in the left sidebar, choose Collect Target Coin Count and set the threshold to 30.

Step 6: Testing & Offline Playtesting

Click ▶ Launch Game in New Tab in the studio toolbar. A new browser tab will open with your compiled game running inside a standalone Blob URL sandbox. Use WASD or Arrow Keys to walk around, collect items, level up, and complete the quest!
